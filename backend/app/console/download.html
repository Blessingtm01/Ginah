Download the AcciSafe apps

Android 8.0 or later. Open this page on your Android phone and tap a download button.

AcciSafe RDC – council reporting app

Report emergencies with GPS and photos, see the live council map, get spoken warnings near incidents, and accident monitoring. Sign in with your council code.

Download AcciSafe RDC (21 MB)

AcciSafe – personal crash alerts

Detects a possible crash or fall, gives you 30 seconds to cancel, then sends your emergency contacts an SMS with your location. Works without mobile data.

Download AcciSafe (6 MB)
How to install
  1. Tap a download button and wait for the file to finish downloading.
  2. Open the downloaded file from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.

These are test versions for demonstration. The apps support, but do not replace, emergency services.
